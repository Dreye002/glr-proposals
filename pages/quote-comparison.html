The Money Question

How to Compare Roof Quotes

The bottom number rarely tells the whole story. The details do.

Two roof quotes can look almost identical on the bottom line and be completely different once you look at what's actually included. The goal here isn't to rush you, it's to make sure you're comparing apples to apples before you choose anyone. Run both quotes through this list.

Check each one on BOTH quotes

What to compareWhy it matters
Same roof area / squaresOne quote may measure less roof than the other.
Tear-off vs. layoverGoing over an old roof is cheaper now, costlier later.
Underlayment typeThe waterproof layer under your shingles. Quality varies a lot.
Flashing (new vs. reused)Old reused flashing is a common leak point.
VentilationPoor ventilation shortens roof life and can void warranties.
Decking / plywood assumptionsIs bad wood replacement included, or a surprise add-on later?
Permit handlingIs the permit pulled and included, or left to you?
Cleanup & magnetic nail sweepProtects your yard, pets, and tires.
Photo documentationDo you get photos of the work, or just take their word for it?
Warranty pathWorkmanship AND manufacturer material warranty, in writing.
Change-order termsWhat happens, and what it costs, if they find more once they start.
License & insuranceLicensed, bonded, insured, and verifiable.
The cheapest quote often wins by leaving things out. If one number is much lower, this list usually shows you why, and it's rarely good news once the roof is off.
What cheaper quotes often leave out: full tear-off (vs. roofing over old layers), new underlayment, new flashing, proper attic ventilation, permits, dump fees, and what happens if they find bad decking once they start. The bottom number rarely tells the whole story. The details do.
