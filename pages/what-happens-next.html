What Happens After You Approve

From final review to the warranty packet, here's the process.

Sometimes the proposal is only half the question. Homeowners also want to know what the project actually looks like once they say yes. Here's exactly how it goes with Green Ladder.

2. Agreement & signatureClear paperwork before work begins.
