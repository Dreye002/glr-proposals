Understanding Your Roof Warranty

Workmanship and material coverage, in plain English.

Roof warranties confuse almost everyone, and that's where some contractors get away with weak coverage. Here's what actually matters, in plain English.

The two warranties on every roof

TypeWhat it covers
Workmanship warrantyThe installation, the labor, how the roof was put on. This comes from the roofer.
Manufacturer material warrantyThe shingles/material themselves. This comes from the manufacturer (like GAF).

Why the installed system matters

Eligible roof systems may qualify for enhanced manufacturer warranty options when installed correctly by a certified contractor. A cheap install can actually void the material warranty, so the lowest bid can cost you coverage.

Why ventilation and flashing matter long-term

Poor ventilation traps heat and moisture and shortens roof life. Reused or sloppy flashing is one of the most common leak points. Both affect whether your roof, and your warranty, actually last.

Your exact warranty depends on the roof system you choose. That's why it's worth walking through carefully rather than guessing, your Green Ladder rep can explain exactly what applies to your proposal.

Questions to ask any roofer

  • Is the workmanship warranty in writing, and for how long?
  • What manufacturer warranty does this system qualify for?
  • Are you certified to offer that manufacturer warranty?
  • Does anything I'm choosing affect the coverage?

Ready when you are

The goal isn't to rush you. It's to make sure you have the right information before choosing a roofer.
